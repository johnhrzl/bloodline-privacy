2. What data we collect

There is no registration, no user account and no sign-in. We never collect your name, email address, phone number or any other detail that identifies you personally.

The app does record how the game is played – but only if you agree to it. See section 6.

3. Where your save game lives

Your save game, settings, achievements and any cosmetic items you unlocked are stored only on your device. They are never uploaded. Uninstalling the app deletes them permanently, and we cannot restore them.

4. The name you type in

The name you give your character stays on your device. It is used to display your character and for nothing else. It is never sent to us or to anyone else.

5. Advertising

The app shows optional video advertisements through Google AdMob. Watching an ad is always your choice – the game never blocks progress behind one.

When an ad is shown, Google processes an advertising identifier, approximate location derived from your IP address, device information and your interaction with the ad. This happens under Google's own responsibility; see Google's privacy policy.

Within the EU, EEA, UK and Switzerland we ask for your consent before the first ad is shown, using Google's consent form. You can choose non-personalised ads there.

6. Analytics

Nothing is recorded unless you say yes. The app asks once, after the tutorial. Until you answer, nothing is collected and nothing is stored on your device for this purpose.

If you agree, the app records:

  • how many lives you start and finish, and what age your characters reach
  • which actions you choose and how you answer in-game events
  • whether you watched an optional advertisement
  • how long a session lasts

This shows us where the game is confusing or unbalanced.

No personal data is involved. We do not record your name, email address, advertising ID, device model or location, and your IP address is discarded rather than stored. Events are tied to a random identifier that is generated on your device, is not derived from anything about your phone or its settings, and changes if you reinstall the app.

Processing is carried out by PostHog on servers in the European Union, acting as our processor under a data processing agreement.

You can change your answer at any time under Settings → Analyse play behaviour. If you switch it off, nothing further is collected, anything still queued on your device is discarded, and the random identifier is deleted.

7. Permissions

The app requests internet access and access to the advertising ID. Both are used for ad delivery only.

The app does not access your contacts, camera, microphone, photos, files or precise location.

8. Children

Bloodline is not directed at children. The game contains simulated gambling and references to alcohol and smoking, and is intended for players aged 18 and over.

9. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to information, correction, deletion, restriction of processing, data portability and to object to processing.

Because analytics events are tied to a random identifier and to nothing else, we have no way of telling which events belong to which person – we therefore cannot look up, export or delete the data of an individual. Switching the setting off stops any further collection and removes the identifier from your device.

For data processed by Google in connection with advertising, please contact Google directly. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles data, this page is updated before the change takes effect, and the date at the top changes with it.
