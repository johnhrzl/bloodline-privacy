2. What data we collect

Bloodline does not collect any personal data. There is no registration, no user account and no sign-in. We do not run any server that receives your data, and we do not sell your data.

3. Where your save game lives

Your save game, settings, achievements and any purchased cosmetic items are stored only on your device. They are never transmitted to us or to anyone else. If you uninstall the app, or choose “Delete everything” in the settings menu, this data is gone for good – there is no backup we could restore.

4. The name you type in

The name you give your character stays on your device. It is used to display your character and nothing else.

5. Advertising

The app shows optional video ads provided by Google AdMob. You decide in each individual case whether to watch one; the game is fully playable without ever seeing an ad. There are no forced or interstitial ads.

To deliver those ads, Google processes the following data:

  • your device's advertising ID
  • your approximate location, derived from your IP address
  • device and usage information

Google is an independent controller for this processing. Their privacy policy is available at policies.google.com/privacy.

Within the EU, EEA, UK and Switzerland we ask for your consent before the first ad is shown. You can withdraw that consent at any time in the app's settings; afterwards you will only receive non-personalised advertising.

6. Permissions

The app requests internet access and access to the advertising ID. Both are used exclusively to deliver the optional video ads. The app does not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone, photos or precise location.

7. Children

Bloodline is not directed at children. The game contains simulated gambling and references to alcohol and smoking, and is intended for players aged 18 and over.

8. Your rights

Because we do not process any personal data ourselves, there is no data held by us that you could request access to, correct or have deleted. All game data is in your hands, on your device.

For the data processed by Google for advertising, please refer to Google's privacy policy linked above, which describes how to exercise your rights towards Google.

9. Changes to this policy

We will update this policy before any change to how data is processed takes effect, not afterwards.
